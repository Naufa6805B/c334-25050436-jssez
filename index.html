03 / Before you expand

Opportunity needs
careful planning.

The playbook describes incentives and practical risks. Check how each point applies to your own operating model.

INCENTIVES

Look at both sides of the border.

The playbook describes targeted incentives in Johor and grants or incentives offered by Singapore agencies. Eligibility depends on activities, location and the programme’s requirements.

Playbook pp. 6, 15
TAX WATCH-OUT / 01

Know where decisions happen.

Repeated business activity or decisions made across the border could create a taxable presence. Map which work happens in Singapore and which happens in Johor.

Playbook p. 14
TAX WATCH-OUT / 02

Price related-party dealings fairly.

When your Singapore and Johor entities trade with one another, the playbook highlights arm’s-length pricing and records that support it.

Playbook p. 14

04 / People and movement

Plan for the
people making it happen.

The playbook’s appendix outlines different routes for expatriate roles, temporary professional work and investment visits.

Playbook pp. 28–29
01

Employment Pass

For approved expatriate positions in eligible activities. MIDA describes key and term posts in the appendix.

02

Professional Visit Pass

For temporary professional services or training on behalf of an overseas employer, including project-based technical work.

03

Investor Pass

For qualifying foreign business travellers visiting Malaysia for investment activities; this is a separate route for decision-makers.
